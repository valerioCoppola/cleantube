# Privacy policy — CleanTube (YourHome)

_Last updated: 3 November 2026_

CleanTube collects nothing. It has no server, no account system, no analytics and no advertising.

**What is stored, and where.** The list of channels you follow, your groups, your settings and the list of videos you marked as watched are saved in your browser's extension storage, on your own device. They never leave it. If you export a backup, the file goes wherever you choose to save it.

**What network requests are made.** The extension contacts only `www.youtube.com`, for these purposes:
- to read the public RSS feeds of the channels you follow (`/feeds/videos.xml`);
- to look up a channel when you add one by link or @handle;
- when you click "Import from YouTube", to read your subscription list. This uses the YouTube sign-in that already exists in your browser. The list is saved locally and sent nowhere else.

Thumbnails and channel pictures are loaded from YouTube's image servers (`i.ytimg.com`, `yt3.ggpht.com`, `yt3.googleusercontent.com`), as on any page that shows YouTube videos. When you open a video, you are on youtube.com, and Google's privacy policy applies there.

**Data sharing.** No data is sold, shared or transferred to anyone, the developer included.

**Contact.** Questions and reports: open an issue at https://github.com/valerioCoppola/cleantube/issues
